A square bracket or curly brace that gathers a column or row of elements; takes no label. Attached to a group, matrix or tokens node it is as long as that side, and an edge leaves from the middle of its spine (the tip of a brace). Structured layout only. A tone colors it with variant outlined.

  • default
  • emphasis
  • muted
  • outlined
since
0.10.0
engines
["structured"]

{"type":"outline","renderer":"sumi/bracket","params":{"form":"square","points":"east"}}
proptyperequireddescription
form"square" | "curly"nosquare: a square bracket (the default). curly: a curly brace with a pointed tip.
points"east" | "west" | "north" | "south"noThe side the spine or tip is on, where edges leave: east gathers the elements to its west (the default), west those to its east, south those above it and north those below it.
widthstringnoExplicit width as a length such as "10pt" (a multiple of 0.5pt). Omitted means 6pt deep for east / west, or as long as the attach target's side (24pt without one) for north / south.
heightstringnoExplicit height as a length such as "40pt" (a multiple of 0.5pt). Omitted means as long as the attach target's side (24pt without one) for east / west, or 6pt deep for north / south.

One figure, every theme × profile

The same source rendered with --theme and --profile overrides: nothing is duplicated. Profiles do not scale a figure unless its source opts into fit: scale. The paper profiles share their spacing and differ only in the width limit that the geometry lint checks, so their drawings match. A profile whose minimum font size is larger than a theme's font sizes rejects the figure before rendering (profile/font-too-small): presentation-16x9 requires 14pt text, so it accepts only the slides theme, and the other themes are not rendered there by design.
